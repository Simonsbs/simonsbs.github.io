From discovery to a production cloud platform

An enterprise cloud opportunity for a customer whose disconnected inventory and operational systems limited visibility and created manual work.

My role
Lead solution architect, Flame-Ware Solutions
Delivery stage
Production
Customer
Anonymised
THE CUSTOMER PROBLEM

What needed to change.

Disconnected inventory and operational systems made it difficult for the customer to see what was happening across the business. Manual work and operating costs were part of the business case for change.

MY RESPONSIBILITY

What I owned.

Led discovery, compared architecture options, prepared the technical proposal and built prototype demonstrations. Worked with customer stakeholders and suppliers on integration dependencies, risks and phased delivery.

CONSTRAINTS & PRIORITIES

What shaped the approach.

  • Business stakeholders wanted better visibility, lower costs and early value.
  • Technical evaluators needed confidence in integration complexity, data consistency, security, scalability and maintainability.
  • Customer systems, supplier dependencies and implementation risks had to be understood before committing to a delivery approach.
ARCHITECTURE & DELIVERY

How the work fitted together.

  1. 01

    Understand the current systems

    Discovery with business and technical stakeholders established the operational problem and integration dependencies.

  2. 02

    Evaluate the proposed solution

    Architecture alternatives, a technical proposal and prototype demonstrations gave stakeholders a basis for evaluating the approach.

  3. 03

    Deliver in phases

    The approved proposal progressed through a phased implementation into a production cloud platform.

TECHNICAL JUDGEMENT

Decisions and responsibilities.

Evaluate options against both sets of priorities

The proposal brought business goals together with the technical team's concerns about integration, security and ongoing maintenance.

Use prototypes during evaluation

Demonstrations supported the technical proposal and discussions with customer stakeholders and suppliers.

Phase the implementation

The delivery approach balanced early business value with technical validation and manageable implementation risk.

THE RESULT

What was delivered.

The approved proposal progressed into production, improving operational visibility, reducing manual processes and supporting lower operating costs.
