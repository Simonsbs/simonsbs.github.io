04 / ENGINEERING JUDGEMENT

Why it works this way.

Feasibility before scoring

An attractive score cannot override a hard boundary, ownership, region, delivery-time or budget constraint.

Keep assumptions editable

Rates, latency baselines, capability ratings and delivery allowances remain visible workshop assumptions. Currency is a label, not an exchange-rate conversion.

Preserve authored judgement

A changed brief flags proposal text for review rather than silently replacing the user's wording with a regenerated draft.

05 / INSPECT THE EVIDENCE

Tested behaviour.
Explicit limits.

  • 48 unit tests and 12 scenario/export checks are published, covering six baseline/changed-constraint decisions.

  • Recorded checks independently reconcile cost totals, budget boundaries, ZIP extraction and JSON round trips.

  • Windows/Linux CI, public browser checks and hosting validation are recorded in the repository.

Read the recorded validation

Scope of this project

Customers, rates, capability ratings and requirements are fictional workshop fixtures.

Exports are proposed drafts and future PoC acceptance criteria, not a completed PoC, binding quotation or delivery commitment.

Drafts remain in this browser origin. Export before switching scenarios or clearing browser storage; no server-side draft store or AI inference is used.
