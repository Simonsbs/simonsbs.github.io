Math For Maya support

Quick answers for parents, guardians and teachers.

Who is Math For Maya for?

The app is designed for children ages 6 to 8 who are practising column addition and subtraction.

Does the app need internet access?

The maths activities work offline and do not require an account. An internet connection is only needed to open the optional website, privacy, data safety and support links.

Does it contain ads or purchases?

No. There are no adverts, in-app purchases, subscriptions or paid promotions.

How do I turn off the applause sound?

Open the menu in the top-left corner of the app and switch Sound off. Visual celebrations still appear.

Does it save scores or progress?

No. Exercise choices and answers are used only in the current app session and are not sent to BestDev.

How do I report a problem?

Email the device model, Android version, app version and a short description of what happened. Please do not include a child's personal information.
